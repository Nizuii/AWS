# How to build a VPC?

In this documentation we are going to look on how to build a VPC from scratch. For this demonstration I will be choosing Mumbai as the region. So lets start with creating a new VPC. Our first task is to sign into our AWS account and here I am using my free tier AWS account. After logging into the account our next step is to VPC category in the AWS services.
